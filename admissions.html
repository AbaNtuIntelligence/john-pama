WELCOME HOME

Enrolment for 2027 is Now Open

John Pama Primary School welcomes learners from Grade R to Grade 7. We are a public, no-fee school (Quintile 2) committed to providing excellent education rooted in the South African CAPS curriculum. Admission is open to all children regardless of race, religion, or background — guided by the Constitution of South Africa and the South African Schools Act.

Age Requirements

Grade R: 4 turning 5 by 30 June
Grade 1: 5 turning 6 by 30 June
Other Grades: Age appropriate per CAPS

School Fees

No-fee school (Quintile 2)
No tuition fees required. Minimal voluntary contribution for activities.

Language Policy

LoLT: English (First Additional Language)
Home Language: isiXhosa
All learners receive instruction in both.

HOW TO APPLY

Application Process

Follow these five steps to secure your child’s place

1

Collect Application Form

Visit the school admin office (Sithandathu Avenue, Nyanga) to collect a hard copy, or download here.

2

Complete & Attach Documents

Fill in all sections clearly. Attach certified copies of the required documents (see list below).

3

Submit in Person

Return the completed application pack to the school office. Deadline: 31 August 2026 (for 2027 academic year).

4

Await Placement Offer

The SGB Admission Committee reviews applications and offers placement based on capacity, proximity, and feeder zone priorities. Parents receive written notification.

5

Accept Offer & Register

Accept the placement offer in writing and complete final registration at the school. Welcome to the John Pama family.

PREPARE YOUR APPLICATION

Required Documents

For the Learner

  • Birth certificate (certified copy)
  • Immunisation / Clinic card (Road to Health)
  • Latest school report (if transferring)
  • Transfer card / Last report from previous school

For Parent/Guardian

  • ID document (certified copy)
  • Proof of residence (recent municipal bill or affidavit)
  • Proof of income / grant letter (if applicable)
  • Two passport-size photos of the learner
All copies must be certified within the last 3 months. Bring original documents for verification during submission.

Admission Policy Summary

  • In line with SA Schools Act & SGB policy
  • Priority: Sibling, feeder zone, then proximity
  • Inclusive education – no learner turned away
  • Late applications waitlisted

What We Look For

We believe every child has a gift. Our admission process considers the whole child — we don't administer entrance exams for Grades R–3. For higher grades, we conduct a baseline assessment to support placement.

Children of all abilities are welcome.

Download the application form or visit our admissions office. Our friendly staff is here to guide you.
