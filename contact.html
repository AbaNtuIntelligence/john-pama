Find Us

We're located in the heart of Nyanga, easily accessible from the N2 highway.

Getting here:

Taxi routes from Cape Town CBD: Take Nyanga taxis from Bellville or Cape Town station.

MyCiTi Bus: Route T01 to Nyanga Junction, then 5-minute walk.

Parking available at the school gate on Sithandathu Avenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enrol my child?

Visit our Admissions page or collect an application form from the school office.

Can I tour the school?

Yes! School tours are available by appointment. Call our office to schedule.

How can I volunteer?

We welcome reading helpers, sports coaches, and mentors. Fill out our contact form or call us.

Do you issue tax certificates?

Yes, for donations over R100. We are a registered PBO (Section 18A).
