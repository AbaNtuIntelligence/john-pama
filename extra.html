HOLISTIC DEVELOPMENT

More Than a Curriculum

At John Pama Primary, we believe education extends far beyond textbooks. Our extracurricular programmes build confidence, teamwork, discipline, and joy. From the dusty playfield to the netball courts, every child finds a place to shine.

⚽ Our Sporting Spirit

Soccer, netball, athletics, and traditional games — pride in every play.

Soccer Academy

U11 & U13 teams compete in local leagues. Practice: Tue/Thu 14:30–16:00.

Netball Stars

Our girls' teams have won 3 consecutive district trophies. All grades welcome.

Athletics

Track, field, cross-country. Annual sports day brings the whole community together.

Arts, Culture & Clubs

Choir & Marimba

Visual Arts

Chess Club
