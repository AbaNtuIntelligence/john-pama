CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK

CAPS – Excellence for Every Child

National Curriculum

Full compliance with DBE CAPS standards (Gr R – Gr 7)

Holistic Development

Cognitive, social, emotional & physical growth

Continuous Assessment

Formal & informal assessment throughout the year

The Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement (CAPS) is the national curriculum framework for South African schools. At John Pama Primary, we implement CAPS with dedication, adapting it to our learners’ lived experiences in Nyanga while maintaining the highest academic standards. Our learners are prepared for the Annual National Assessments (ANAs) and the transition to high school with confidence and competence.

GROWING MINDS

Foundation Phase (Grades R – 3)

Laying the Cornerstones

Our Foundation Phase focuses on literacy, numeracy, and life skills. We teach in English with isiXhosa as Home Language, ensuring cultural relevance. Small class sizes and individual attention build confidence.

ASSESSMENT

How We Measure Progress

Continuous Assessment

Classwork, homework, projects, tests & examinations (Gr 4–7).

Progress Reports

Formal reports issued quarterly; parent-teacher conferences twice a year.

Promotion (Gr 7)
