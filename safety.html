THE REALITY WE FACE

Confronting the Crisis

Nyanga faces some of the highest rates of gang violence and drug abuse in the country. But at John Pama, we refuse to let these challenges define our children's futures.

60%

of Nyanga youth report exposure to gang violence

1 in 4

800+

OUR COMMITMENT

How We Protect & Empower

We are not just a school — we are a fortress of hope, a sanctuary, and a launchpad for brighter futures.

Peace & Conflict Resolution

Peer mediation programs and restorative justice circles to resolve conflicts without violence.

Substance Abuse Prevention

Regular workshops, counselling, and awareness campaigns about the dangers of drugs and alcohol.

Safety & Security Measures

Controlled access, trained security personnel, and safe passage protocols for learners.

Life Skills Education

Age-appropriate curriculum teaching resilience, decision-making, and personal safety.

Psychosocial Support

On-site social workers and trauma-informed care for affected learners and families.

Positive After-School Activities

Sports, arts, and clubs to keep children engaged in safe, constructive environments.
