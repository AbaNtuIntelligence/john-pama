OUR HERITAGE

A Brief History

Founded in the heart of Nyanga, Cape Flats — one of Cape Town's oldest townships established in 1968 — John Pama Primary School has stood as a beacon of hope for generations.

The Proud School Of John Pama Primary School Based In South Africa, Has A Fantastic Repution For Excellence In Education. The School Body Is A Trusted Pillar Of The Community..

Through apartheid, forced removals, and the struggle for liberation, John Pama Primary never closed its doors. Today, it serves over 800+ learners and continues to produce doctors, teachers, engineers, and artists who give back to Nyanga and beyond.

OUR COMPASS

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing, inclusive, and academically excellent environment where every child discovers their potential, embraces their heritage, and is empowered to break the cycle of poverty through education.

— No child left behind —

Our Vision
